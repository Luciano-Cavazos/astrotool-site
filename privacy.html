AstroTool Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026

AstroTool is an astronomy planning and session logging app for iOS. This policy explains exactly what data the app accesses, what it stores, and what — if anything — leaves your device.

What AstroTool Does

AstroTool helps you plan observing sessions, log your captures, manage your equipment, browse a catalog of 284 deep sky objects and 63 double stars, and track upcoming sky events. All astronomical calculations (moon phase, twilight times, object altitude, rise and set times, planet positions) run entirely on your device using standard algorithms. No internet connection is required for any of this.

Data We Access

Data Why Leaves your device?
Location (while app is open) Used on-device to calculate twilight times, object altitudes, moon rise/set, and rise/set times for your specific position on Earth. No — never transmitted anywhere.
Device motion & compass Used by the Sky Map to orient the view based on where you point your phone, and to display a live compass heading. Processed locally in real time. No — never transmitted anywhere.
Notifications (optional) If you enable alerts on the Events screen, the app schedules local notifications on your device for upcoming astronomical twilight and meteor shower peaks. All scheduling is done locally; no server is involved. No — local notifications only, no remote push.
Object images When you view a deep sky object, AstroTool fetches a thumbnail image from Wikimedia Commons (a public, non-profit media repository). The request contains no personal data — only the name of the image file. Images are cached on your device for future offline use. The image filename only, sent to Wikimedia Commons' public CDN. No user data is included.

Data You Create

AstroTool stores the following on your device only, using Apple's SwiftData framework (local SQLite database):

  • Observation sessions — date, location name, Bortle scale, seeing, transparency, notes
  • Logged objects — target name, exposure details, sub count, gain, filter, equipment, rating, notes
  • Target schedule items — planned captures with times and parameters
  • Equipment library — telescopes, cameras, mounts, eyepieces, filters
  • Catalog favorites
  • Session plan checklists

None of this data is transmitted to us or any third party. It is never uploaded to a server, synced to a cloud service, or shared in any way unless you explicitly choose to do so (see "Sharing & Siri" below).

Sharing & Siri

AstroTool integrates with a few iOS system features. Each is user-initiated and processes data on-device:

  • Session sharing. The share button on a session detail produces a plain-text summary of that session and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes (Messages, Mail, Notes, etc.). AstroTool does not transmit anything itself.
  • Clipboard. The copy buttons on the catalog detail view and calculator (coordinates, field of view, exposure values) write the selected text to your device's clipboard so you can paste it into another app.
  • Siri & Shortcuts. AstroTool exposes two on-device Siri shortcuts: "Get tonight's targets" and "Get twilight times." Both compute their answers entirely on your device. Apple's Siri handles the voice recognition itself, under Apple's privacy policy.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reports sent to external services
  • No data sold, rented, or shared with any third party

Third-Party Services

The only external service AstroTool communicates with is Wikimedia Commons, solely to fetch publicly available deep sky object images. Wikimedia's own privacy policy applies to those requests. AstroTool contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting SDKs.

Image Credits

Deep sky object images are sourced from Wikimedia Commons and are credited to their respective creators — including NASA, ESA, Hubble Space Telescope, ESO, JWST, and individual astrophotographers — as displayed in the image credit overlay within the app. Images are used under their respective public domain or Creative Commons licenses.

Children's Privacy

AstroTool does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data Deletion

All data created in AstroTool is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app.
