AstroTool Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

AstroTool is a personal astronomy and astrophotography planning app. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data We Access

Location

AstroTool requests access to your device's location while the app is in use. This is used exclusively to calculate the positions of stars, planets, and other sky objects as seen from your location. Your location is never transmitted, stored on any server, or shared with any third party. It is used only locally on your device in real time.

Device Motion

AstroTool uses your device's gyroscope and motion sensors to orient the sky map based on where you are pointing your phone. Motion data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted or stored.

Observation Logs

Any observing sessions, notes, or equipment records you create within the app are stored locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No personal information (name, email, account)
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reporting sent to external services
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted anywhere

Third-Party Services

AstroTool contains no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, or advertising frameworks.

Children's Privacy

AstroTool does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
