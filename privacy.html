ASTROTOOL Privacy Policy Effective date: May 2026  ·  Last updated: May 2026 Short version: AstroTool collects nothing. No account, no tracking, no analytics, no ads. Your location is used only on-device to calculate the sky above you and is never transmitted anywhere. Your session data lives only on your device. 1. Information We Collect AstroTool does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically: No name, email address, or account is required or requested No usage analytics or crash reporting services are integrated No advertising SDKs are included No third-party tracking of any kind is present in the app 2. Location Data AstroTool requests access to your device location for one purpose only: to calculate the positions of stars, planets, the Sun, and Moon as seen from where you are standing. Location is used entirely on-device — it is never sent to any server Location is never stored beyond the current app session You can deny location access; the app will use a default location with reduced accuracy AstroTool uses iOS location permission level When In Use. It never requests background location access. 3. Data You Create The Session Logger lets you record observing sessions, capture details, and equipment profiles. This data: Is stored only on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework Is never uploaded, synced to any server, or accessible by us Can be exported as plain text at any time using the share button in the Session Logger Is deleted when you delete the app 4. In-App Purchases AstroTool offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features. All purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not process, store, or have access to any payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all purchase-related data. 5. Notifications AstroTool may request permission to send local notifications for upcoming astronomical events (meteor showers, oppositions, conjunctions, moon phases). These notifications are: Scheduled entirely on-device — no server involvement Never used for marketing or promotional purposes Fully controllable via iOS Settings → Notifications → AstroTool 6. Children's Privacy AstroTool does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, there is nothing to distinguish by age. 7. Changes to This Policy If this privacy policy changes materially, the effective date at the top of this page will be updated. We will never introduce data collection, advertising, or tracking without clearly disclosing it here first. 8. Contact Questions about this privacy policy?
