Frequently Asked Questions

Why are my times wrong?

AstroTool uses your device's GPS location to calculate sunrise, sunset, and twilight times. Make sure Location Services are enabled for AstroTool in Settings → Privacy → Location Services. Times are displayed in your device's local timezone.

Why does it show San Francisco as my location?

That's the default fallback when location access hasn't been granted. Enable location access in Settings and reopen the app.

How are the Best Targets scored?

Each object is scored using a combination of its current altitude (60% weight) and angular separation from the Moon (40% weight). Higher scores mean better imaging conditions for that night.

How many objects are in the catalog?

AstroTool includes 284 deep sky objects spanning the Messier, Caldwell, NGC, and IC catalogs.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. All astronomical calculations are performed on-device. No internet connection is required.

The Sky Map isn't working — is that a bug?

The Sky Map feature is currently under active development and will be available in a future update.
